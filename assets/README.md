# Profile image sources

- `scientific-ml-banner.png`: generated by `scripts/generate_banner_v2.py` from SBSI galaxy stamps, analytic probability contours, and a seeded Zel'dovich-style LSS projection.
- `forklens-calibration.png`: combined from the unweighted and weighted FORKLENS calibration figures used in the research statement.
- `blendemu-pair-response.png`: combined from `example_0.png` and `example_1.png` in the BlendEMU repository.
- `sbsi-measurement-flow.png`: resized from `examples/measurement_flow_contours.png` in the SBSI repository.
- `skyvar-validation.png`: resized from the spatial-redshift posterior-predictive-check figure used in the research statement.

The scientific plots show project results. The banner is a reproducible scientific illustration; its galaxies come from SBSI simulations, while its contours and LSS field are code-generated.
